| Research Question | Scientific question | VCNet (arXiv 2508.02995) demonstrates that a CNN built on the macro-organization of the primate visual cortex — hierarchical processing, dual-stream segregation, top-down predictive feedback — achieves state-of-the-art accuracy on Spots-10 (92.1%) with claimed gains in data efficiency and robustness. VCNet incorporates no microcircuit mechanism. The Drosophila mushroom body provides exactly such a mechanism: winner-take-all sparsification via the APL neuron, which gates Kenyon-cell responses so only the strongest hypothesis survives. Question: **does adding MB-style microcircuit sparsification (per-position channel top-k) to the VCNet macro-skeleton improve adversarial robustness at zero accuracy cost?** |
| Research Question | Why this is scientific research rather than pure engineering optimization | The hypothesis concerns whether a biologically-derived inductive bias (mushroom-body winner-take-all gating) provides a benefit that generic regularization does not, at matched parameter count — not parameter tuning. All three arms share identical data, augmentation, optimizer, epochs, and (for the two VCNet arms) identical architecture except the activation function. The comparison is pre-registered: accuracy within noise across arms; robustness ordered kwta > relu > plain. The result either way answers whether macro-structure and microcircuit principles compose, which neither the VCNet paper nor the k-WTA literature has tested. |
