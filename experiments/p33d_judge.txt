## Reasoning

We treat each discovery in the given chronological order and ask: how much does its parameter pair `(x0, lambda)` depart from the structure already seen?

The early discoveries establish the reference structure:

- `N=3`: `(0.08333333, 0.25)`
- `N=5`: `(0.05, 0.25)`
- `N=2`: `(0.10696938, 0.23670068)`
- `N=7`: `(0.03125, 0.23809524)`
- `N=11`: `(0.01379931, 0.21156557)`
- `N=13`: `(0.00961538, 0.2)`

By this point, the search has seen:
- several rational/simple values of `x0`,
- `lambda` near `0.25`, then drifting downward,
- a general trend of decreasing `x0` and decreasing `lambda`.

Later discoveries are ranked by how much they break or extend this pattern.
